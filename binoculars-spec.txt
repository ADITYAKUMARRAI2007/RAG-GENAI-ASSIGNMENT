TRD Binoculars Specifications

The specific details about the specifications of binoculars for TRD uses are as follows:

1. **Magnification**: Minimum 12x
2. **Objective Lens Diameter**: Minimum 50mm
3. **Field of View at 1000m**: Minimum 90m
4. **Prism Type**: BaK-4 (Roof)
5. **Prism Glass Features**: Dielectric Prism
6. **Lens Coating**: Fully Multi-Coating
7. **Weight**: 600-1000g
8. **Waterproof/Fog proof**: Yes, IPX7 minimum
9. **Focus**: Center + Diopter
10. **Sun Glass Filter for Use in Desert Areas**: Yes

Overview:
These specifications ensure that the binoculars provide a stable, clear image, are comfortable for handheld use, and are suitable for monitoring and inspecting OHE assets and transmission lines.

Key Performance Characteristics:
- The 12x magnification with a 50mm objective lens provides excellent light gathering capability and zoom range
- BaK-4 prism with dielectric coating ensures superior image quality and brightness
- Fully multi-coated optics reduce glare and reflections for clearer viewing
- IPX7 waterproofing rating ensures durability in harsh field conditions
- Center focus with diopter adjustment allows precise vision correction for extended viewing sessions
- The 600-1000g weight range keeps the binoculars portable for extended field work
- Sun glass filter is essential for desert operations where bright sunlight can cause eye strain
